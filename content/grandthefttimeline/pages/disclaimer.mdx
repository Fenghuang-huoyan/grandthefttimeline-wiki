This site does not sell Grand Theft Timeline, process purchases, redeem codes, or recover accounts. It is not responsible for purchase decisions, for Early Access changes after release, or for store text that shifts after a sentence here was written. The planned Steam date cited here is 19 November 2026. On 12 November 2026 the store still marked the game coming soon.

Nothing here is account support. The Steam store page lists the website https://www.tbjbu2.com/ and the email grandemprise@gmail.com. That site and that mailbox are the ones printed for the developer. Mail sent there does not reach the people who edit this wiki, and this wiki does not read that mailbox.

The guide quotes dated Steam text. Where the store has not named a world, a weapon, a price, or a code, the page says so instead of filling the gap. Counts such as 200+ worlds, 25+ theme packs, and 2,000+ NPCs are the store’s marketing claims. Steam store artwork is used to illustrate these pages and is credited in the attribution file next to the images. The site’s favicon is original and is not a REAL PLAYERS or Valve logo.

A “checked” date is the day the source was read, not a promise that the sentence is still true in a future client.

The privacy note for this static site is on [Privacy](/privacy). A shorter copy of this disclaimer is in the footer of every page.
