Steam has not published a list of the worlds in **Grand Theft Timeline**. This page keeps the sentences that do exist, then records HUD text that is actually readable in six official screenshots. A frame is not a level select.

## What the feature list says about worlds

**200+ worlds.** The heading is “200+ worlds.” The sentence under it says real history and pure fiction share one time machine, “from the dinosaurs to Squid Game.” The short description says “200+ worlds of history & fiction.” Both lines are the store’s claim. This site has not opened a world select and counted entries.

**25+ theme packs.** “Play one like a story, world by world, all the way to its finale.” The store does not name a pack, a chapter count inside a pack, or the order of finales.

**2,000+ NPCs.** The capture bullet says you knock them down and drag them home in cages. It does not name them. This page will not supply names the store omitted.

**Per world, three repeated promises.** Guns and special weapons: every world has its own to find. A boss in every world: unlock the map to it, then the store says you kill it, or cage it and take it home. Valuables: steal that world’s treasures and sell them for cash. No map legend, drop table, or boss name follows those sentences.

**Workshop worlds.** A separate bullet says you can download community worlds or make your own with the mod kit included with the game. Community worlds are not part of the 200+ claim unless a later post says they are. The two trailer titles on the app, “Authored Worlds Trailer (Non Modded)” and “Modded Trailer,” are names of videos. This page does not treat a title as a table of contents.

<Callout kind="note" title="One span example, not a route">
Dinosaurs and Squid Game are the two ends the store itself prints. Everything between them in a fan list would be invention. Screenshot labels below are extra evidence of a kind: pixels in a marketing image, dated to the file this site saved.
</Callout>

## What a “world” is asked to contain

From the bullets, a world is a place you arrive in with the time machine, where you can find that place’s weapons, meet a crowd, steal valuables, and reach one boss after the map to the boss is unlocked. You can leave with a caged capture. The departure rule — the machine leaves with whoever is inside — is on the [guide](/guide) and the [co-op](/coop) page. It is a rule about the trip, not the name of a world.

The home base, the garage, and the timeline arena are described as places you return to, not as entries in the 200+ count. The arena line says captures fight there “in a Roman colosseum” to unlock the next worlds. That is the store’s picture of the arena. It is not a promise that Rome is one of the indexed worlds, and it is not a floor plan.

## Workshop, and the gap

The mod bullet is the only place the store invites worlds from outside the shipped set: download them, or build them with the included kit. No kit documentation was in the appdetails text. No discussion of a world editor’s file format belongs on this page until a source prints it.

Still absent after the 12 November check: a named index, a theme-pack list, a count that this site verified in a client, a boss roster, and a weapon roster. The [progression](/progression) page says what the store claims you unlock by moving through those worlds. It does not add the missing names.
