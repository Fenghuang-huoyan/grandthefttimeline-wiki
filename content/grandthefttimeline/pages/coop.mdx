**Grand Theft Timeline** is not out. A co-op tip written on 12 November 2026 can only point at the Steam categories and the feature sentences. Nothing below is a latency preset, a host menu, or a role the client has confirmed.

## What the store lists for players

| Line | Where it appears |
| --- | --- |
| 1–4+ players | Short description, and the “1–4+ players” feature bullet |
| Online co-op, and solo is fun too | The same player bullet |
| Single-player, Multi-player, Co-op, Online Co-op | App categories |
| Steam Cloud, Family Sharing | App categories, not described further |
| Broadband internet connection | Windows minimum spec |

The “4+” is the store’s punctuation. It does not state a maximum of 5, 8, or any other integer. This page will not invent one. Family Sharing and Steam Cloud are listed beside the player modes. The payload does not say whether a shared copy can join the same online session.

Solo is explicitly included: “solo is fun too.” A co-op tip page that ignored that line would misread the store. You can plan a group, and the same bullet says a single player is a supported way to play.

<Callout kind="note" title="Not a network guide">
No dedicated-server box, cross-play line, or voice setting was in the text. The broadband row is the only network requirement printed. If the client adds a party menu, that menu is newer than this page.
</Callout>

## Tips that are really store rules

These are coordination notes a group can take from the copy. Each one names the sentence it comes from.

**Leave together.** “The machine leaves with whoever’s inside. Miss it and you show up decades older. Or as a baby.” The store is describing a penalty for missing departure, not a bug. A group that splits up to grab one more capture is taking the risk that sentence names. This site cannot say how long the machine waits, because no timer is printed.

**Expect the other player to touch the controls.** The about text says every button on the time machine does something, “and your friend is pressing all of them.” That is a premise sentence, not a control diagram. The practical reading is that the machine is a shared object. The page does not list the buttons.

**Split a ride if the store’s ride bullet is in the build.** “Ride em while your friends shoot from the back.” One person is on the mount or vehicle, and the others fire. The store does not name the rides, the seats, or what happens if you are alone. Solo play is still listed, so a ride is not described as a required party action.

**Crowds are written as a group problem.** The crowd bullet talks about hordes and gives the example “100 one-hit-mummies coming for you at once.” Treat the 100 as the store’s example, not as a tested spawn. The heist page on the [guide](/guide) keeps the same sentence next to weapons and throws. Throwing is “grab whatever’s lying around.” No combo is printed.

**Captures are a shared haul.** You knock NPCs down and drag them home in cages, and the garage is expanded “to fit more captures.” The store does not say whether a cage belongs to one player or to the crew. The paradox those captures produce is the fuel on the [progression](/progression) page. A group that fills the garage is following the only capacity sentence the store gives, and that sentence has no slot count.

**Stocks spend the crew’s cash.** The stocks bullet is “gamble the crew’s cash.” If that system ships as written, one player’s bet is the group’s money. No opt-out is described. That is a reason to agree before anyone opens it, and it is not a stock tip.

## What this page refuses to add

Host migration, ready checks, friendly fire, a revive, a ping button, and a recommended party size inside the “1–4+” range are all unpublished. So is any region or language requirement for matchmaking. The twelve languages in `supported_languages` are a store string, not a co-op filter.

The [worlds](/worlds) page is where HUD labels live. They are not co-op objectives. A year on a screenshot is not a rendezvous point until a source says the group must meet there.

When the 19 November 2026 build is actually playable, a useful co-op note will cite the client or a post from REAL PLAYERS and will keep the date. Until then, the departure rule and the ride sentence are the two tips the store has already written down.
