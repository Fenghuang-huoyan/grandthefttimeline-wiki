This is a short English wiki for **Grand Theft Timeline**, Steam AppID **4928450**, built as a static site for **grandthefttimeline.wiki**.

The game’s full title is used on every page. The site does not rebrand the game, and it does not speak for REAL PLAYERS.

## What the pages cover

The [guide](/guide) is the store overview: the time-travel heist, captures, the garage, and the PC spec. [Worlds](/worlds) keeps the 200+ claim, theme packs, and HUD labels from six screenshots. [Progression](/progression) lines up paradox, the arena, and garage upgrades without inventing a tree. [Co-op](/coop) keeps the 1–4+ player lines and the departure rule.

There is no codes page. The Steam text checked on 12 November 2026 does not describe a redeem-code system.

<Callout kind="note" title="What “checked” means">
“Checked 12 Nov 2026” means the Steam API and the English store page were read that day. It does not mean someone finished a launch build. `coming_soon` was still true, and the printed date was 19 Nov, 2026.
</Callout>

## Images

The library hero, the library capsule, the store header, the capsules, and the screenshots are official Steam store assets for AppID 4928450. They were downloaded from the Steam CDN and are served from this site so pages do not hotlink Steam. The file list and source URLs are in [ATTRIBUTION.txt](/images/official/steam/ATTRIBUTION.txt).

The favicon is an original clock mark drawn for this site. It is not a REAL PLAYERS logo and it is not a Valve logo.

## Privacy and the disclaimer

Analytics and advertising are described on [Privacy](/privacy). Affiliation, the support mailbox, and trademarks are on the [disclaimer](/disclaimer). A short copy of the disclaimer is in the footer of every page.

## What is still unpublished

- A numeric Steam price. The 12 November appdetails payload had an empty package list, and `is_free` was false.
- A recommended spec beyond “64-bit processor and operating system.”
- A named world index, a weapon table, an NPC roster, and any upgrade cost.
- Whether the 19 November build keeps the store’s wording about cages, paradox, and the machine leaving without you.
