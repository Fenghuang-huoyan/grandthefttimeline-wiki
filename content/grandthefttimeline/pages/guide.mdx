## What Steam says the game is

The short description on the store:

> 1-4+ players time-travel kidnapping-collection dungeon-crawling heist with physics, guns, bosses, arena, homebase, skins, and stocks! Kidnap people across 200+ worlds of history & fiction, haul them home in cages and farm paradox in your garage. 25+ theme packs, 2,000+ NPCs and Workshop mods!

The longer text calls it a 1–4+ player co-op time-travel heist across 200+ worlds. You and your friends built a time machine. The store says you use it to rob people, and that every button on it does something. Captures go home in cages. The home base is also called a garage, and the store says the captures there produce paradox that fuels the machine.

## The heist loop, as separate bullets

The feature list is a set of headings, not a numbered mission. Read together, they describe one outing and the return home. [Progression](/progression) keeps the between-trip parts. This section is the outing.

**Worlds and theme packs.** The store says real history and fiction share one time machine, and gives the span “from the dinosaurs to Squid Game” as its example. A theme pack is played “like a story, world by world, all the way to its finale.” No pack names are printed in that paragraph. Screenshot labels are collected on [Worlds](/worlds) and are not promoted to a catalog.

**Captures.** The store says there are 2,000+ NPCs to capture. You knock them down and drag them home in cages. The short description uses the same cage-and-garage sentence. This page does not add a capture method, a health value, or a list of names.

**Weapons.** “Every world has its own special weapons to find.” The store also says you can grab what is lying around and throw it. It does not print a weapon table, ammo counts, or which gun belongs to which world.

**Bosses.** “A boss in every world.” You unlock the map to it, then the store says you kill it, or cage it and take it home. No boss names are in that sentence.

**Valuables.** Steal each world’s treasures and sell them for cash. No price list is attached.

**Crowds and rides.** The store describes hordes, and gives one example: “100 one-hit-mummies coming for you at once.” That is the store’s crowd example, not a bestiary. Rides are a separate bullet: someone rides while friends shoot from the back.

**Do not miss the machine.** The machine leaves with whoever is inside. The store says that if you miss it you show up decades older, or as a baby. That line is the departure rule. Co-op consequences are repeated, briefly, on [Co-op](/coop).

**Home, paradox, arena, upgrades.** Captures live in cages at home and “drip paradox.” You vacuum it up, feed them, and the store says they get fat and make more paradox to fuel the machine. The timeline arena is where captures fight, in the store’s words “in a Roman colosseum,” to unlock the next worlds. Between trips you upgrade the time machine, buy guns, and expand the garage so it holds more captures “to get further in the timeline.” Stocks are a way to gamble the crew’s cash. Skins cover outfits and gun skins from the worlds you visit, and the store says you can shrink a capture into a pet. None of those bullets include a cost.

**Workshop.** Download community worlds, or make your own with the mod kit that comes with the game. The store says no skill is required. This site has not opened the kit. Steam’s appdetails payload lists two trailers by title: “Authored Worlds Trailer (Non Modded)” and “Modded Trailer.” This page does not summarize footage it has not used as a written source.
